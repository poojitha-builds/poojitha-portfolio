PROJECTS

Smart Safety Helmet for Electrical Line Workers

An MSME-funded safety solution designed to improve the protection of electrical line workers operating in hazardous environments. The helmet integrates multiple sensors to detect unsafe working conditions, monitor worker status, and provide instant alerts during emergencies. Features include fall detection, electric shock detection, gas sensing, GPS-based location tracking, emergency SOS communication, and real-time health monitoring. The objective is to reduce workplace accidents, improve emergency response time, and enhance worker safety through embedded electronics and IoT technologies.
